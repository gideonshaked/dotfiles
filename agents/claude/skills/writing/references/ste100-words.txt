# ASD-STE100 Simplified Technical English, Issue 9 (January 2025), Part 2, Dictionary.
# One line per entry, sorted by word. Fields are separated by " | ":
#
#   word (part of speech) | verdict | approved meaning | use instead
#
# The verdict is "approved" or "not approved". A dash means the field does not apply.
# For an approved word, "use instead" gives what to use for its other senses, and is a
# dash when the word carries only the one meaning. For a word that is not approved, the
# field gives the approved words to use, or the standard's guidance where it gives no
# substitute. (TN) marks a technical noun and (TV) a technical verb, which name a
# category for your own terms rather than a specific word.
#
# Look a word up with its trailing space, so the match is the entry and not a mention
# of the word somewhere else:
#
#   grep -i "^work " ste100-words.txt
#
# The full standard, including the writing rules and the example sentences left out
# here, is at https://www.asd-ste100.org/
